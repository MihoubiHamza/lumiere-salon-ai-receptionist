    Privacy policy

    This policy explains how Hamza Mihoubi, self-employed, processes personal data when clients chat with Yasmine on WhatsApp, Messenger or a website on behalf of a salon.

    Data processed

    • The client's WhatsApp number or Messenger ID, and their name.
    • The content of the messages and voice messages sent to the assistant.
    • Appointment details: date, time and the service requested.

    Use

    This data is only used to reply to the client, manage their appointments with the salon concerned and hand the conversation over to the salon team when needed. It is never sold or used for advertising.

    Technical providers

    The service relies on providers to work: WhatsApp and Messenger (Meta) for messages, Google for the calendar and the conversation log, and artificial intelligence services to understand written and voice messages. Data is shared with them only for this purpose.

    Demo chat on this website: messages typed in the demo chat at the top of this page are sent to Hamza Mihoubi's server, then to the artificial intelligence service that generates Yasmine's replies. They are only used to produce these replies. Please do not enter personal information.

    Retention

    Data is kept as long as needed to manage appointments and the relationship between the client and the salon, then deleted. Demo bookings made on this website are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

    Your rights

    You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to the e-mail address in the Contact section.
