What Yasmine takes care of, day and night.

She handles the repetitive messages, so your team can focus on the clients in the chair.

  • Bookings that fit the service

    She only offers times that are really free for the whole service, from a 30-minute cut to a 3-hour balayage, and writes the appointment in your Google Calendar.

  • Changes and cancellations

    Clients move or cancel by message. The freed time is immediately available for someone else.

  • Your services, prices and policies

    Cuts, colour, balayage, treatments, nails, facials: she answers with your own price list, durations, opening hours and policies, never with made-up numbers.

  • Voice messages

    Many clients prefer to talk. On WhatsApp and Messenger, Yasmine transcribes voice notes in your salon's main language and replies in writing.

  • English, French, Spanish, Arabic

    She detects the client's language from each message, casual spelling included, and replies naturally in that language.

  • Hands over to your team

    A complaint, a reaction after a treatment, a special request: she alerts your team on WhatsApp instead of improvising.

Frequently asked questions

Does Yasmine replace my receptionist?

No. She takes care of repetitive messages and requests outside opening hours. Your team keeps the cases that need a person, and Yasmine hands them over.

Can clients choose a specific stylist?

Today Yasmine books into one shared salon calendar: each time slot holds one appointment. She can note a stylist preference in the booking for your team, but she does not manage separate schedules per stylist.

What if a client asks about an allergy or a reaction?

Yasmine never gives medical or skin-care advice. She tells the client to seek medical help if it is urgent and alerts your team so a person can follow up.

I already use booking software.

Yasmine works with Google Calendar today. If your salon uses other software, we look together at the best setup before the trial.

What happens to my clients' data?

It is only used to reply to clients and manage their appointments. It is never sold or used for advertising. Details are in the privacy policy at the bottom of the page.

How much does it cost?

The 2-week trial is free. After that, the monthly subscription depends on your salon's message volume, and we agree on it together after the trial.

Privacy policy

This policy explains how Hamza Mihoubi, self-employed, processes personal data when clients chat with Yasmine on WhatsApp, Messenger or a website on behalf of a salon.

Data processed

  • The client's WhatsApp number or Messenger ID, and their name.
  • The content of the messages and voice messages sent to the assistant.
  • Appointment details: date, time and the service requested.

Use

This data is only used to reply to the client, manage their appointments with the salon concerned and hand the conversation over to the salon team when needed. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Technical providers

The service relies on providers to work: WhatsApp and Messenger (Meta) for messages, Google for the calendar and the conversation log, and artificial intelligence services to understand written and voice messages. Data is shared with them only for this purpose.

Demo chat on this website: messages typed in the demo chat at the top of this page are sent to Hamza Mihoubi's server, then to the artificial intelligence service that generates Yasmine's replies. They are only used to produce these replies. Please do not enter personal information.

Retention

Data is kept as long as needed to manage appointments and the relationship between the client and the salon, then deleted. Demo bookings made on this website are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to the e-mail address in the Contact section.
